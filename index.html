    Frequently asked questions

    What is the 67 Personalities assessment?

    The 67 Personalities assessment is a six-axis questionnaire that produces a profile built from six independent preferences. Each result is a code representing the dominant pole on every axis, together with a descriptive profile of the corresponding type.

    Why is the first question the same for every respondent?

    The first question serves as a standard anchor for all assessments. It ensures that every profile begins from a common reference point.

    How is my data stored?

    Your answers and results are stored only in your browser's local storage. They are not transmitted to any server. Clearing your browser storage will remove your history.

    Can I retake the assessment?

    Yes. You may retake the assessment as many times as you wish. If you believe your assigned type is inaccurate, you may submit a request through the Contact form under the topic "My type is wrong".
